\section{What this supplement holds}
\label{supp:about}

This supplement accompanies \emph{Six Birds Foundations V: Endogenous Closure --- A Catalog of
Structural Laws for Living, Cognitive, and Social Systems}. The main paper states and proves every
result; its numbered items are cited here by their numbers there. The supplement gives the Lean
counterpart of each numbered item and the status of each entry in the theorem catalog of the
development (\Cref{supp:concordance}); the fields of every certificate (\Cref{supp:fields}); the
register of non-implications with their separating examples (\Cref{supp:nonimpl}); the finite
computations (\Cref{supp:evidence}); the life-facing synthetic data and a proposed biological
protocol (\Cref{supp:life}); the axioms used by the Lean development (\Cref{supp:status}); and the
commands that rebuild everything (\Cref{supp:replay}).

Every declaration named in \Cref{supp:concordance,supp:status} is present at the file and line
given; the tables of those two sections are generated from the Lean sources.
