\section{Non-implications}
\label{supp:nonimpl}

\Cref{tab:nonimpl} lists, for each entry, statements that the results do not support, with the
separating example or the missing hypothesis. A \emph{separating example} is a record that satisfies
the stated hypotheses and fails the tempting conclusion; the examples marked \emph{finite} are
realized in the finite computations of \Cref{supp:evidence}, and the others are stated here.
\Cref{tab:exclusions} lists the exclusion statements checked in Lean: each says that, for one
claim, raw evidence of a higher-priority kind excludes a set of lower judgments.

Each exclusion is proved from explicit negations of higher-priority evidence in the lower branches
together with uniqueness of the status for one claim. None of them derives the completeness of an
inventory, the honesty of a comparator, a source classification or a causal identification.

Refining the quotient raises the predictive surplus. & Refinement can worsen a closure score; the
surplus inequalities need their comparison and chain-rule hypotheses (\Cref{prop:d2}). \\
The device produced its own history. & The history and its associations are declared with the
device; \Cref{thm:e3-episode} checks that they agree with the executed step. \\
The link in \Cref{thm:e3-episode} carries information about which defect was repaired. & The defect
sort of the device has one element; a richer defect model is needed (\Cref{sec:scope-open}). \\
Every finite E-system has a bounded, non-final self-model. & The capacity bound needs a declared
measure (\Cref{thm:e2-declared}). \\
An audited repair forces a new audit level. & The order of the level tags and the next level's audit
relation are a certified input (\Cref{sec:capacity}). \\
E2 gives an optimal audit depth. & Only with a cost and error model. \\
The budget spend equals the value of the allocation. & They are separate data; the multiplier
witness of \Cref{thm:e6-kkt} names neither an objective nor a feasible set. \\
The individual of \Cref{thm:e12-repair} is found from the dynamics alone. & Maximality is relative to
the declared boundary regions (\Cref{def:e12-setting}). \\
The argmax of \Cref{thm:e9} is what the system chooses. & The theorem constructs a maximizer; a
policy's choice is shown optimal by its own argmax certificate. \\
A strict acquisition installs a repair. & Only a later record linking it to an obstruction does. \\
Offline phases are necessary whenever debt grows faster than online capacity. & Necessity is part of
the E15 certificate; the scalar deficit alone does not give it, and $a \le \kappa_{\mathrm{on}}$
alone does not give online sufficiency (\Cref{tmpl:e15}). \\
A skipped-offline cascade shows that a scheduled phase was skipped. & It records an all-online
deficit with growing debt, a timed alarm and later stress (\Cref{tmpl:e15}). \\
Unequal repair probabilities after two routes show that a route creates capacity. & The E16 record
reports the difference; a causal reading needs an intervention bridge (\Cref{tmpl:e16}). \\
The internalization record constructs the repair family. & The family is a field of the endogenous
branch (\Cref{tmpl:e1}). \\
A compiled repair is brittle on every other class. & \Cref{prop:e4-brittle} certifies only an
exhibited split pair. \\
The alarm Template shows that a carrier must alarm. & The disposition is supplied by the package
(\Cref{tmpl:e7}). \\
A decodable internal state is cognitive. & A readable state without certified difference-making is
a decodable correlate (\Cref{tmpl:e10}). \\
Repair, gate and provenance evidence together give the cognitive status. & The six higher-priority
kinds of evidence must also be absent (\Cref{tmpl:e10}). \\
A signal that tracks a constraint is its shadow price. & Linkage, held-out prediction, stability and
slack collapse are required (\Cref{def:e8}). \\
A long run without collapse shows viability. & Collapse is relative to the declared rescue inventory
and viability probes (\Cref{def:e5}). \\
A structural downward path is an accepted channel. & The blocked-channel witness imported from
\FoundIII{} separates them (\Cref{tmpl:e11}). \\
A compiled repair is constitutive. & No theorem maps an E4 compilation to the constitutive status of
E11. \\
Transport across two contexts gives symbolic repair. & Symbolic repair asks that a token reactivate
a carried repair in every context of a declared family; \Cref{thm:e13-cross} transports a repair
relation, not a token (\Cref{tmpl:e13}). \\
Force and an empty census give a coercion null. & The absence of committed receiver repair and an
unchanged obstruction are separate conditions (\Cref{tmpl:e13}). \\
A changed record after retrieval is a silent rewrite. & Only with no lawful outcome, no typed
disposition and no mutation provenance (\Cref{tmpl:e14}). \\
