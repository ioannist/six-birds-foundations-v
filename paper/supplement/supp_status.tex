\section{Formal status}
\label{supp:status}

\subsection{The library}

The Lean~4 library has six modules for the definitions D1--D6, fifteen for the entries E1--E16 (E6
and E9 share one) and twenty for the instances. It uses the vendored Lean interfaces of \FoundIII{},
of the adequacy-residual development and of the holonomy development, imports no other external
library, contains no \texttt{sorry}, and declares no axioms of its own. The first-version capacity
measure of E2 and the statements that use it are part of the library; the main paper states which
of them are vacuous.

\subsection{Hypotheses carried but not used}

Several Lean statements of the Templates carry a
hypothesis that their proof does not use; each Lean statement is then a special case of what its
proof establishes. The cases are: in E1, closed-loop scope and finite forcing in the internalization
classification; in E2, the audited-repair trigger of forced stratification and the ordered tower
listing in the declared bound; in E6, the binding budget of \Cref{thm:e6-kkt}; in E9 and the
arbitration statements, positive costs; in E4, the window's obstruction reduction and idempotence
stability, and $C' \ne C$ in \Cref{prop:e4-brittle}; in E5, the prior status and the withdrawal event;
and in E7, the blind-spot, coupling, policy and active-family hypotheses. The printed statements omit
these hypotheses.
