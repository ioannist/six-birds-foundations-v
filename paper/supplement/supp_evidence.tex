\section{Finite computations}
\label{supp:evidence}

\subsection{The finite carriers}

Repair-World is a family of finite controlled Markov carriers whose states have the form
\[
  z = (y, q, L, r, \Lambda, A),
\]
with a public state $y$, a carried quotient table $q$, an active probe family $L$, a record ledger
$r$, a budget vector $\Lambda$ and one or more carried audit levels $A$. The environment supplies a
finite challenge process with controllable recurrence, drift, novelty and viability coupling. The
common kernel implements the carrier, source, quotient, probe, ledger and audit interfaces; E4, E14,
E15 and E16 use their own finite checkers over the same vocabulary, on rings of 18, 20 and 30 states,
a probe shop and a memory wheel. The computations use exact rational arithmetic. For each entry the
expected outputs --- statuses, counts, rational values and the controls that must be rejected --- were
fixed before the checker was run, and a comparison passes only on equality with the fixed value.

The computations show that the hypotheses of each certificate can be met on a finite carrier, that
every status of each classification has a constructed case, and that the constructed false positives
are rejected. They do not show that any catalog of probes or rescues is empirically complete, that
any domain reading is an instance, or that the frequencies of statuses generalize.

The capacities and footprints in the E2, E3 and E8 computations are record counts computed
directly on the finite carriers. They are not instances of either Lean capacity measure.

\subsection{Values by entry}

\paragraph{E1.} The challenge is at baseline at times $0$--$1$ and splits at $2$--$4$. The initial
obstruction has one pair and the installed join removes it. The carrier with its repair generator
spends $2$ units of a budget of $3$ and is endogenously repairing at times $2$--$4$. With the
generator removed the statuses are stressed, stressed, collapsing, with residuals $1, 2, 3$. External
subsidy and a schedule trap both remove the obstruction but leave one endogeny defect and are
classified externally subsidized; a same-family relabel leaves the obstruction and is stressed.

\paragraph{E2.} On a carrier of capacity $8$, towers of depth $0$--$4$ have footprints
$0, 2, 5, 8, 10$, so depths up to $3$ fit. Triggered audit rotates depth $1 \to 2 \to 3$; the
saturated trigger stays at $3$. The fresh levels have tags $20$ and $30$ and targets $\{0\}$ and
$\{0, 1\}$; a level with the wrong next index is rejected. With loss equal to audit cost plus repair
error, the losses at depths $0$--$3$ are $18/24, 14/24, 13/24, 14/24$, so depth $2$ is optimal for
this loss.

\paragraph{E3.} Without challenge the apparatus distance is $1/8$ under tolerance $1/4$. After the
maintenance channel is cut, the apparatus distance is $0, 1/8, 3/8, 1/2, 5/8, 3/4$ and the object
distance $0, 1/16, 1/16, 1/8, 3/16, 5/16$; they separate first at time $2$, while the maintained
reference stays at $1/8$. A crystal-grade control never separates. Absent-record, fallback,
off-kernel, out-of-scope, uncarried and audit-omission controls each make the maintenance
obstruction nonempty.

\paragraph{E4.} Two candidates, an operator rewrite and a support gate, pass all gates and are
compiled. Adding an out-of-class witness makes one mis-compiled; adding a decompilation event makes
one decompiled. A blocked channel, a rejected promotion, a memory-only reproduction, same-family
saturation and unverified descent each give compiling. A one-invocation window and a P6 compiled
sort are rejected at construction. A window that reduces the obstruction while its payload drifts
fails stability, and one that is stable without reducing the obstruction fails reduction.

\paragraph{E5.} An $18$-state ring realizes all seven statuses; repair, boundary update, acquisition
and apparatus reclosure each supply a separate viable rescue. External rescue gives subsidized while
the internal inventory is still collapsed. A carrier with an active kernel and no rescue move is
collapsed-recoverable. An over-budget descending move is rejected as a falsifier. After withdrawal of
a subsidy the scenario is classified viable once absence of active subsidy is checked.

\paragraph{E6 and E9.} In the probe shop the exact optima at budgets $1, 2, 3$ are $(4/5, 1/5)$,
$(7/5, 3/5)$ and $(2, 1)$ with equal selected-probe ratios $25/9$, $25/16$ and $1$; budget $4$ is
slack at $(2, 1)$ with price zero. Discharge rises $14/3 \to 27/4 \to 8 \to 8$. A proxy allocation
discharges $2$ at every budget and at budget $3$ leaves residual trace $45/4$ against $21/4$. The
largest excess direction is probe A with magnitude $3$ and moves to probe B with magnitude $8$ after
the declared change of factor. Finite differences at perturbation $1/20$ give selected ratios
$100/37, 8/3$ at budget $1$, $75/49, 50/33$ at $2$ and $60/59, 40/39$ at $3$, with spreads that shrink
along perturbations $1/5, 1/10, 1/20$. Strict candidates C, AC and BC each discharge $1/4$, with
ratios $1/4, 1/8, 1/16$; same-family candidates discharge nothing. Joint arbitration spends budgets
$1$--$3$ on allocation and acquires C only at budget $4$, raising total discharge from $8$ to $33/4$.

\paragraph{E7.} Residual and excess are $0, 1, 4, 9$ for hazard levels $0$--$3$. Monitoring budgets
$1, 2, 3$ give detection latencies $2, 1, 0$, and budget $0$ never currentizes. In three hazards,
preemption selects the alarm action and survives three times, while the old ratio ranks the ordinary
action at $1$ above the alarm action at $1/2$ and fails three times. Protocol artifacts at thresholds
$0$ and $1$ are discounted. Boundary nonclosure and alarm fatigue are not exercised.

\paragraph{E8.} The binding component spends its unit budget with multiplier $3/2$; a slack
component spends $1/2$ with multiplier $0$; a slack signal of $1/4$ is obstructed. A proxy with
multiplier $4/5$, signal $9/10$ and drift $1/5 > 1/20$ fails alignment and stability. Genuine discharge
lowers the residual from $5$ to $2$; the capture case raises the budget from $10$ to $15$ with a
positive original-lineage blind spot and a healthy report; a rise from $10$ to $20$ with the residual
falling from $9$ to $4$ is not capture.

\paragraph{E10.} All eight cognitive, five intention and four goal statuses are realized. Controls
reject a support comparison linked to the wrong intervention, a repair credited to the wrong class, a
post-outcome restriction, an approximate support shift, an incoherent future probe, a reward-only
explanation, an omitted route evaluation, and uncarried intention or goal records.

\paragraph{E11.} A support rewrite and a closure-only rewrite are realized, with all four statuses.
A weight change from $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-2}$ is conditioning. A support difference with a blocked
channel is inert. The constitutive status appears only with the compiled-rewrite record.

\paragraph{E12.} On a $30$-state ring: integrated candidates; a subsidiary with closure signature
(repair, budget, record) $= (T, F, T)$; a platform-dependent candidate $(T, T, T)$ whose boundary is
not self-maintained; a shadow $(T, T, F)$; federated fragments and overlapping maximal candidates. A
candidate with all three closures and no sufficient boundary is non-individuated, and a smaller
closing candidate is rejected when a declared larger one closes. A two-class boundary is sufficient
and coarsest; a three-class one is sufficient but not coarsest.

\paragraph{E13.} Clean communication, teaching with the source absent, coercion null, stable symbolic
reactivation, scaffolding, priming and different-class influence are realized. Eight failure
controls --- unpaid bridge, uncarried receiver repair, role change, context instability, role drift,
saturation relabel, a parameter-only channel, a flow crossing with no bridge --- are transport
rejected.

\paragraph{E14.} Under a loose memory budget the outcome counts (repair, coarsen, unresolved) are
$(6, 1, 1)$; under a tight one $(1, 6, 1)$. This interaction with the budget is observed, not derived.
One source record is repaired for one retrieval claim and unresolved for another. A duplicate context
or two transports for one context reject the setup; wrong-direction and non-strict coarsening fail.

\paragraph{E15.} A lawful alternation has observed and predicted duty cycle $1/4$; a mismatch has
$1/3$ against $1/4$, a gap of $1/12 > 1/24$. The debt from three sources sums to $20$; the budget
identity is $6 = 4 + 2$, and fitted gains $11/2$ and $13/2$ are rejected. At accrual rates $7/2$ and
$4$ the claim is online-sufficient, and at $5$ alternation is required.

\paragraph{E16.} In the memory-wheel carrier the two endpoints are current-event equivalent and
$p_\gamma = 3/4$, $p_\eta = 1/4$ for the repair challenge. The current loop action is trivial and the
predictive action is not; $256$ continuations are enumerated. One case of each of flat, artifact,
flattenable, currentizable, dissipative and coherent is realized. A perturbation of $3/4$ lies outside
the declared bound $1/2$; an in-bound perturbation of $1/4$ destroys coherence and gives rejection.
Stored probabilities that disagree with the counted ratio are rejected.
