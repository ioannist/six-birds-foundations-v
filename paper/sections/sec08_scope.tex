\subsection{Domain readings}\label{sec:scope-domains}

A domain name does not instantiate an entry. \Cref{tab:domains} lists, for each entry, candidate
readings in living, cognitive and social systems, and the data an application must supply before a
reading becomes an instance. The readings are candidates for testing; none is claimed as an
instance here. Every application must in addition answer the common questions: which finite
states or histories are compared and at what scale; which quotients are current, predictive or
target-side, with a literal split pair for each failure of descent; which instrument, ledger,
repair, boundary and probe records are coordinates of the carrier and updated by its kernel; which
resource is charged and in what units; which source produced each credited record and which
external, fallback, off-kernel or wrong-lineage records are excluded; and which controls were
run. Three guards apply throughout. Alarm is preemption, curiosity is strict acquisition and goal
is persistent target selection; the words attribute no feeling or purpose. A ledger may be
energetic, material, computational, financial or legal, and calling it a budget does not make these
convertible. Individual, lineage, population, organization and ecosystem are different carrier
choices, and evidence at one does not establish carriedness at another.

The atlas shows two things. Carried repair, audit capacity, priced sensing and apparatus
maintenance are biological from the first entries on; life does not first appear at E12 or E16.
And the cognitive column is not a promotion of the living one: a living system may or may not meet
the E10 criterion, and every other entry is meaningful either way.

\subsection{What the catalog leaves out}\label{sec:scope-deferred}

Several candidates have a recognizable shape but no distinct obstruction or theorem yet: global or
conscious access, which may combine E6, E7, E12 and E13; play, which currently reads as E9 under a
gated risk budget; the search for boundaries, which E12 classifies but does not produce; transport of
gates and norms rather than repairs; distributed price discovery across carriers; reproduction,
heredity and population selection; and the origin of a first E-system from non-living chemistry. The
catalog defines neither life nor death, pathology or welfare.

\subsection{Open problems}\label{sec:scope-open}

The sorting of \Cref{tab:catalog} suggests the following problems, each of which would move an entry
to a stronger status.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Stratification from a construction rule.} Derive the order of the level tags and the
    next level's audit relation of E2 from a rule for adding a level, rather than supplying them, and
    restate the regress stop of E3 and the meta-audit bound of E8.1 over the declared measure.
  \item \emph{Richer instances.} Replace the single-valued record sorts and the declared completeness
    propositions of the repair device by a model in which defects, inventories and formedness have
    content, so that the history link of \Cref{thm:e3-episode} and the audit of \Cref{thm:e2-tower}
    carry information.
  \item \emph{Optimality beyond capped linear objectives.} Extend \Cref{thm:e6-caps} to $n$ probes,
    general costs and a wider class of concave discharge objectives, and derive the spend of the
    ledger from the objective.
  \item \emph{Open-ended novelty.} Give an instance of \Cref{prop:e1-forcing} in which novelty and
    strictness are measured on the obstruction the challenge causes, rather than on a clock.
  \item \emph{Individuals and transport.} Add budget and record closure and a boundary derived as the
    coarsest viability-sufficient quotient to \Cref{def:e12-setting}; prove that a maximal candidate
    exists above every candidate; and add carried sending and receiving records to
    \Cref{def:e13-transport}, with an instance of teaching.
  \item \emph{Route effects.} Supply an intervention bridge under which the unequal repair probabilities
    of E16 are an effect of the route.
\end{enumerate}
